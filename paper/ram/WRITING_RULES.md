# Writing rules — arXiv preprint

1. **No em dashes (—).** Use commas, parentheses, colons, semicolons, or split the sentence.
2. **Use an accessible research tone:** motivate every design choice from a real-world problem and write in first-person plural ("we").
3. **Write for a broad robotics reader.** Explain the task and mechanism in plain terms before technical detail.
4. **Ground every claim in real sources:** `sections/`, `supplementary/`, actual results and figures. Flag uncertain numbers instead of inventing them.
5. **Word budget ~4500 words** for the main paper. Draft expansively while addressing reviewer comments, then trim. Avoid needless verbosity.
